# Add Garage Opener Widget to Your iPhone

Multiple ways to quickly access your garage door opener from your iPhone home screen and lock screen.

## Option 1: Home Screen Icon (Easiest - 30 seconds)

Add a one-tap button to your home screen:

### Steps:
1. Open **Shortcuts** app
2. Find your **"Open Garage"** shortcut
3. Tap the **•••** (three dots) in the corner
4. Tap the icon/color at the top
5. Choose an icon (🚗 car, 🏠 house, or 🚪 door work well)
6. Choose a color
7. Tap **Done**
8. **Long press** the shortcut
9. Select **"Add to Home Screen"**
10. Name it: **"Garage"** (or whatever you want)
11. Tap **Add**

Now you have a one-tap garage opener on your home screen!

---

## Option 2: Home Screen Widget (Shows Status)

Add an interactive widget to your home screen:

### Small Widget (shows one shortcut):
1. **Long press** empty space on home screen
2. Tap **+** (top left)
3. Search for **"Shortcuts"**
4. Select **"Shortcuts"** widget
5. Swipe to **Small** size
6. Tap **"Add Widget"**
7. **Long press** the widget once placed
8. Tap **"Edit Widget"**
9. Tap **"Shortcut"** and select **"Open Garage"**
10. Tap outside to save

### Medium Widget (shows 4 shortcuts):
Same steps, but choose **Medium** size. Great if you also want:
- Open Garage
- Close Garage (create separate shortcut)
- Turn on lights
- etc.

---

## Option 3: Lock Screen Widget (iOS 16+)

Open garage without unlocking your phone:

### Steps:
1. **Long press** your lock screen
2. Tap **Customize**
3. Tap the lock screen preview
4. Tap one of the widget areas below the time
5. Search for **"Shortcuts"**
6. Select a **Shortcuts** widget
7. Choose **"Open Garage"** shortcut
8. Tap **Done**
9. Tap **Done** again

Now just tap the lock screen button when you arrive home!

---

## Option 4: Action Button (iPhone 15 Pro/16 Pro)

Use the Action Button to open garage:

### Steps:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Action Button**
2. Swipe to **Shortcut**
3. Tap **Choose a Shortcut**
4. Select **"Open Garage"**
5. Tap **Done**

Now hold the Action Button to open your garage!

---

## Option 5: Back Tap (Any iPhone)

Open garage by tapping the back of your phone:

### Steps:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Accessibility**
2. Tap **Touch**
3. Tap **Back Tap**
4. Choose **Double Tap** or **Triple Tap**
5. Scroll down and select **"Open Garage"**

Now double/triple tap the back of your iPhone to open garage!

---

## Option 6: Siri Voice Command

Just ask Siri:

### Setup:
1. The shortcut is automatically available to Siri
2. Just say: **"Hey Siri, Open Garage"** or **"Hey Siri, Open My Garage"**

You can also add it to Siri suggestions:
1. Settings → Siri & Search → Shortcuts
2. Find "Open Garage"
3. Enable **"Show in App"** and **"Suggest Shortcut"**

---

## Option 7: Control Center Button

Add to Control Center for quick swipe access:

### Steps:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Control Center**
2. Scroll down to **More Controls**
3. Find **"Shortcuts"** and tap the **+** icon
4. Now swipe down from top-right (or swipe up on older iPhones)
5. Long press the **Shortcuts** button
6. Tap **"Open Garage"**

---

## Option 8: CarPlay Button (If You Have CarPlay)

Add to your car's screen:

### Steps:
1. Connect to CarPlay
2. Open **Shortcuts** on your car screen
3. Your "Open Garage" shortcut will appear
4. Tap it while driving home

Or set up automatic trigger:
1. Settings → Shortcuts → Automation
2. Create automation: **"When CarPlay Disconnects"**
3. Action: **Run Shortcut → Open Garage**
4. This opens garage when you disconnect from CarPlay (arriving home)

---

## Option 9: Apple Watch Complication

Control from your watch:

### Steps:
1. Open **Watch** app on iPhone
2. Tap **My Watch** → **Shortcuts**
3. Enable **"Open Garage"**
4. On your Apple Watch, add Shortcuts complication:
   - Long press watch face
   - Tap **Edit**
   - Add **Shortcuts** complication
   - Select **"Open Garage"**

Or just:
- Raise your wrist and say **"Hey Siri, open garage"**

## Bonus: Create "Close Garage" Shortcut

Duplicate your shortcut for closing:

1. Long press **"Open Garage"** shortcut
2. Tap **Duplicate**
3. Rename to **"Close Garage"**
4. Edit the shortcut
5. Find the last URL action (action 6)
6. Change the URL to: `https://api.myqdevice.com/api/v6.0/accounts/me/devices/actions/close`
7. Done!

Now add both to a Medium widget for full control.
